Result Explanation Page

How the result is calculated

Risk score

Combines model probability with structural evidence such as suspicious tokens, redirects, brand similarity, homograph indicators, and domain reputation signals.

Confidence score

Measures agreement between model certainty and independent feature evidence. Higher confidence means the verdict is supported by multiple signals.

Trusted-domain handling

Known legitimate domains are kept safe when HTTPS, reputation, domain structure, and URL behavior look normal. Deceptive evidence overrides this protection.
